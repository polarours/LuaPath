## How to Get Listed Here

1. **Make a contribution** via pull request
2. **Include your name** in the PR description (or request anonymity)
3. **Maintainers will add you** to this file upon merge

### Contribution Types

We recognize these contribution categories:

- **Core Contributors**: Multiple significant contributions, ongoing involvement
- **Chapter Authors**: Wrote new chapter content or major rewrites
- **Translation Contributors**: Translated chapters or improved translations
- **Bug Reports**: Reported significant errors or inaccuracies
- **Content Review**: Technical review, fact-checking, clarity improvements
- **Infrastructure**: Scripts, CI/CD, tooling, automation

---

## Recognition Levels

### 🌟 Founding Contributors

Contributors who helped shape the project in its early stages (before v1.0).

### 📖 Chapter Sponsors

Contributors who authored or significantly rewrote entire chapters.

### 🔧 Tooling Contributors

Contributors who built infrastructure, scripts, or automation.

### 🐛 Bug Hunters

Contributors who found and reported significant errors.
